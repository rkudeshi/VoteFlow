Sort by any column. Percentages are shares of the precinct's voters; turnout is voters over registered voters.

About these numbers. Voters counts everyone credited with voting, including provisional voters whose ballots were not counted, so it runs slightly above ballots counted. Mail means mailed absentee ballots counted by election night; counted after Election Day means mail ballots that arrived by the deadline and were counted later. Hand-counted mail ballots are included with mail.

The early-voting line uses each day's absentee list, so its last pre-election point can differ slightly from the final count. Polling places are each election's Election Day locations; none are listed for November 2023. Precinct lines are today's, so a few older precincts may not line up exactly.
